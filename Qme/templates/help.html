Getting Started

New here? This is the fastest way to go from opening the app to finishing your first level.

1

Open the app

Open index.html in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. No sign-up or installation required.

2

Go to the Level Trail

Click Level Trail in the top navigation to see all levels. Locked levels are greyed out; your current level is highlighted.

3

Start a level

Tap an unlocked level, review the level info in the popup, then click Start Level to begin the timed test.

4

Answer & submit

Use the question-jump grid at the bottom to move between questions. Click Submit Test when you're done, or let the timer run out.

5

Check your results

Your score, grade, and full answer review appear right after submitting. Head to Calendar anytime to see your history.

02

Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to expand the answer.

How many questions and how much time does each level have?

Each level has 15 questions to answer within 30 minutes. The timer starts as soon as you click Start Level and auto-submits the test when it reaches zero.

How do I unlock the next level?

Score 40% or higher on a level's test to unlock the next one. You can retake a level as many times as you like to improve your score or try to unlock the next stage.

What topics are covered in the questions?

Every test mixes three areas: quantitative aptitude (percentages, averages, profit/loss, etc.), logical & relationship reasoning (blood relations, coding-decoding, series, etc.), and real-world coding questions in C++, Java, and Python.

Does the difficulty change as I go up in levels?

Yes. Levels are grouped into four difficulty tiers — Foundations, Building Speed, Advanced Reasoning, and Placement-Ready — so questions get progressively harder as you climb the trail.

Will my progress be saved if I close the browser?

Yes, as long as you're opening the same file in the same browser on the same device. Your level progress, scores, and attempt history are stored locally so they're there when you come back.

Can I go back and review my answers after a test?

Yes. On the results screen, click Review Answers to see every question again along with your answer and the correct one, side by side.

What do the letter grades mean?

Grades are based on your percentage score: 90%+ is A+, 75–89% is A, 60–74% is B, 40–59% is C, and below 40% is F (not cleared).

Does this platform need an internet connection?

No. Once the files are on your device, the platform runs fully offline in your browser — no server or internet connection required to take tests.

03

Troubleshooting

Running into a problem? Try these fixes first.

📄

Page looks unstyled or broken

Make sure index.html, style.css, and script.js (or about.css / help.css for those pages) are all in the same folder. If a linked file is missing, the page loses its styling.

⚠️

Windows shows a "Security Warning" popup

This happens if you double-click a .js file directly. Click Cancel and open the .html file instead — that's the one meant to be opened in a browser.

🔒

A level won't unlock

You need at least 40% on the current level's test to unlock the next one. Check your best score for that level from the Level Trail popup, and retake it if needed.

💾

Progress disappeared

Progress is tied to the browser and device you used. Opening the files in a different browser, a different computer, or an incognito/private window will show a fresh, empty state.

⏱️

Timer ran out before I finished

The test auto-submits when time hits zero, and unattempted questions are marked as such. Try answering the questions you're confident about first, then come back to the harder ones.

🖥️

Layout looks off on my phone

The platform is optimized for both desktop and mobile browsers. If something looks cramped, try rotating your device or opening the page in a different mobile browser.

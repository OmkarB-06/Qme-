Frequently asked questions

Is Question Me free to use?

Yes — creating an account and using the full 20-level trail is free.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser once you sign up and log in — no downloads or extensions required.

What if I don't clear a level?

You can retake any unlocked level as many times as you like. You need 40% or higher to unlock the next one.

Is my progress saved if I come back later?

Yes — once you're logged in, your level progress, scores, and attempt history are tied to your account.
